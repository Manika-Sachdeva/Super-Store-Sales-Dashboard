# Super Store Sales Forecast Dashboard (Power BI Project)
## Project Objective
The Super Store Sales Forecast Dashboard is designed to analyze retail sales performance and forecast future sales trends using historical order data. The dashboard helps businesses monitor KPIs, understand customer and regional performance, and predict short-term sales for better planning and decision-making.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Manika-Sachdeva/Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard/blob/main/SuperStore_Sales_Dataset.csv">Dataset1</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total sales revenue generated?
- What is the total quantity sold?
- What is the total profit earned?
- What is the average shipping duration?
- Which payment mode contributes maximum sales?
- Which region generates the highest revenue?
- Which customer segment contributes most to sales?
- Which product category and sub-category perform best?
- What are the monthly sales and profit trends?
- Which states generate maximum sales?
- What is the 15-day future sales forecast?

## Process
- Cleaned and transformed raw retail sales data using Power Query.
- Removed missing values and ensured data consistency.
- Built relationships between tables for accurate data modeling.
- Created DAX measures for:
  - Total Sales
  - Profit
  - Quantity Sold
  - Ship Days
- Developed forecasting models using historical order date trends.
- Designed interactive visualizations including maps, donut charts, line charts, and forecasting graphs in Power BI.
- Added slicers and filters for dynamic dashboard interaction.

## Project Insights
- Consumer segment contributes the highest share of total sales.
- COD and Online payment methods dominate transactions.
- Office Supplies category generates the highest revenue.
- Phones and Chairs are among the top-selling sub-categories.
- West region contributes the highest percentage of sales.
- California and New York are top-performing states.
- Sales and profit show significant growth toward year-end periods.
- Forecasting analysis predicts stable future sales trends over the next 15 days.

## Key Features

## Final Conclusion
The dashboard provides a complete overview of retail business performance while enabling future sales forecasting using historical data. It helps businesses identify high-performing regions, profitable product categories, customer purchasing behavior, and seasonal trends. The forecasting feature supports better inventory planning, operational efficiency, and strategic decision-making through data-driven insights.
